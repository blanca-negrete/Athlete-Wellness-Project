Welcome to Athlete Wellness

A simple wellness check-in designed for student-athletes to reflect on their physical and mental well-being.

About the Check-In

Student-athletes are often under a lot of pressure to perform at their best, both on and off the field. Our wellness check-in is designed to help student-athletes reflect on their physical and mental well-being, and provide them with resources to improve their overall health.
