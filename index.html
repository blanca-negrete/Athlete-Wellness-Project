About the Check-In

Being a student-athlete can be challenging. Balancing practices, competitions, academics, recovery, and personal life can affect overall well-being. Athlete Wellness provides a place to reflect on how you are doing, explore helpful resources, set goals, and discover tools that may support your wellness journey.
