Wellness Resources

Explore resources designed to support different areas of student-athlete wellness.

Mental Wellness

Taking care of your mental wellness is an important part of being a student-athlete. Make time for rest, reflection, positive connections, and activities that help you recharge.

  • Take regular breaks during busy days.
  • Stay connected with supportive teammates, friends, and family.
  • Use campus counseling or wellness services when additional support is needed.

Physical Recovery

Recovery helps student-athletes balance training, competition, and everyday responsibilities.

  • Make time for rest between training sessions.
  • Stay hydrated and maintain consistent nutrition.
  • Use appropriate recovery activities after practices and games.

Academic Balance

Managing academics alongside athletics can be challenging. Planning ahead can help create a more manageable schedule.

  • Keep track of assignments and important deadlines.
  • Break larger assignments into smaller tasks.
  • Use academic support services when needed.

Stress Management

Recognizing stress and making time for healthy coping strategies can support overall wellness.

  • Take short breaks during long study or training periods.
  • Practice relaxation or breathing exercises.
  • Make time for activities outside of academics and athletics.

Need Additional Support?

Athlete Wellness is designed for general wellness support and resource discovery. It is not a substitute for professional medical or mental health care.

If you need additional support, consider contacting your university's appropriate health, counseling, academic, or athletic support services.
